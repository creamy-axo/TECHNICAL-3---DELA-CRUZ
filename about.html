About PinoyBigBarks

Founded in Quezon City, PinoyBigBarks started with a simple goal: give Filipino fur parents affordable, safe, and joyful essentials for dogs of every size—from tiny Shih Tzus to big, bouncy bulldogs and Aspins.

Our Promise

Every dog house, bowl, collar, and leash on our shelves is tested for durability and pet-safe materials.

Community First

Part of every purchase supports local shelter feeding drives and weekend adoption days.
